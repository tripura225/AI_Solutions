About Purna Bhandari

Purna Bhandari is the Chief Data Officer at DataFirst, a company specializing in helping businesses leverage their data for competitive advantage. With a PhD in Data Science and 12 years of experience, Purna is recognized as a thought leader in the analytics space.

The Challenge

DataFirst was sitting on massive amounts of customer data but lacked the tools to extract meaningful insights. Traditional analytics tools were too slow and couldn't handle the volume or complexity of their data. They needed a solution that could process data in real-time and provide predictive insights.

The Solution

DataFirst implemented our Smart Analytics platform, which uses AI to analyze complex datasets, identify patterns, and predict future trends. The system was integrated with their existing data warehouse and customized to their specific KPIs.

The Results

  • 25% revenue increase - $2M in new opportunities identified
  • Real-time insights - From 24-hour delay to instant
  • 40% faster decision making - Leadership acts on data faster
  • 95% prediction accuracy - For customer behavior forecasting

What Purna Says

"Smart Analytics has given us a competitive edge we never thought possible. We're now able to predict customer needs before they even express them. The platform is intuitive, powerful, and has become an indispensable part of our operations."
