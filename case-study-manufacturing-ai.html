Executive Summary

This case study examines how MfgPro, a global manufacturing company with 20+ factories, implemented AI-powered predictive maintenance to reduce unplanned downtime, extend equipment life, and save millions in operational costs.

The Challenge

MfgPro was experiencing significant operational challenges:

  • Unplanned Downtime: $10M annual loss from unexpected machine failures
  • Reactive Maintenance: Fixing equipment only after failure
  • High Maintenance Costs: Inefficient use of maintenance resources
  • Production Delays: Missed delivery deadlines affecting customer trust

The Solution

MfgPro deployed our predictive maintenance solution including:

  • IoT Sensor Integration: Real-time machine monitoring
  • AI Failure Prediction: Machine learning models predicting failures 72 hours in advance
  • Smart Scheduling: Optimized maintenance planning
  • Analytics Dashboard: Real-time equipment health monitoring

How Predictive Maintenance Works

  • Data Collection: IoT sensors collect vibration, temperature, and performance data
  • AI Analysis: Models detect anomalies and predict failures
  • Early Warning: Alerts sent 72 hours before predicted failure
  • Preventive Action: Maintenance scheduled during planned downtime

The Results

  • 45% reduction in unplanned downtime - From 15% to 8%
  • $5M annual savings - Through reduced downtime and optimized maintenance
  • 30% reduction in maintenance costs - Better resource allocation
  • 95% prediction accuracy - Highly reliable failure predictions
  • 20% increase in equipment life - Extended asset lifespan

"Predictive maintenance has completely transformed our operations. We now fix machines before they break, not after. The savings have been tremendous."

— Chief Operations Officer, MfgPro

Lessons Learned

  • Start with the most critical equipment first
  • Invest in quality IoT sensors and data infrastructure
  • Train maintenance teams on the new system
  • Combine AI predictions with human expertise
