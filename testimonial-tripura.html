About Tripura Bhandari

Tripura Bhandari is the Director of Operations at ServeHub, a fast-growing customer service platform that helps businesses manage their support operations efficiently. With over a decade of experience in operations management, Tripura has a keen eye for technologies that improve efficiency and customer satisfaction.

The Challenge

ServeHub was handling thousands of customer inquiries daily, but their response times were averaging 4-5 hours. Customers were frustrated with the wait times, and the support team was overwhelmed, leading to burnout and high turnover rates.

The Solution

ServeHub implemented our AI Chatbot solution to handle routine customer inquiries. The chatbot was trained on their knowledge base and configured to handle common questions about billing, account management, and technical support. Complex issues are seamlessly escalated to human agents.

The Results

  • Under 2 minutes response time - Down from 4-5 hours
  • 70% of inquiries handled automatically - No human intervention needed
  • 40% reduction in support costs - Saved over $300,000 annually
  • 95% customer satisfaction rate - Up from 72%
  • 24/7 availability - Support available around the clock

What Tripura Says

"The AI chatbot has completely transformed our customer support operations. Our team is no longer overwhelmed, and our customers are happier than ever. The ROI has been incredible, and we're now looking at expanding the chatbot to handle even more complex queries. I couldn't recommend AI Solutions more highly."
